## What is Intuit Assist?

Intuit Assist is not a standalone product you buy separately — it is the generative AI layer built directly into QuickBooks Online (QBO). Rather than a tool that competes with QuickBooks, it *is* QuickBooks, upgraded. The right framing is that Intuit Assist is a feature of QBO, not something you evaluate in isolation.

Inside QuickBooks, Intuit Assist answers plain-language financial questions, drafts transaction categories, surfaces anomalies, and helps assemble month-end summaries — all grounded in your actual books. By 2026, Intuit has extended the assistant's reach into payroll, reconciliation, and sales tax guidance, making it one of the most comprehensive AI layers in any small-business accounting platform.

## Key Features

- **Natural-language financial queries** — Ask questions like 'What did I spend on contractors last quarter?' and get answers pulled directly from your transactions, no formula required.
- **Draft-first transaction categorization** — Intuit Assist suggests categories for uncategorized entries but always drafts for your approval before posting. This is a deliberate design choice that meaningfully reduces the risk of AI errors hitting your books.
- **Month-end narrative summaries** — Generates plain-language summaries of P&L, cash flow, and flagged anomalies to speed up review cycles.
- **Sales Tax AI** — Available on higher-tier QBO plans (Plus and Advanced), this flags potential sales tax obligations based on transaction geography.
- **Reconciliation support** — Surfaces discrepancies and suggests matches during bank reconciliation, cutting manual review time.
- **Payroll AI assistance** — With a QuickBooks Payroll add-on, AI agents flag anomalies and can draft payroll runs for approval.

A meaningful differentiator vs. competitors like Xero JAX and Zoho Zia: Intuit Assist consistently operates in draft mode, proposing actions rather than executing them autonomously. For owners who want AI speed without surrendering oversight, that tradeoff is exactly right.

## Pricing

Intuit Assist is included with every QuickBooks Online subscription at no additional charge — there is no separate AI add-on fee. QBO plans start from approximately $30–38/mo at regular pricing (Intuit frequently runs promotions such as 90% off for the first three months). Higher-tier plans unlock advanced AI capabilities, including Sales Tax AI on Plus and Advanced.

One real limitation: lower-tier QBO plans carry a 25-query-per-month cap on AI interactions. Heavy users who want to use Intuit Assist as a daily assistant rather than an occasional lookup tool will feel that ceiling quickly and should evaluate Plus or Advanced before committing.

Always verify current plan pricing and query limits directly at quickbooks.intuit.com, as Intuit adjusts tiers and promotions regularly.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- No extra cost — bundled with your existing QBO subscription
- Draft-first approach keeps a human in the loop before anything posts
- Answers are grounded in your actual books, not generic financial advice
- Covers tax, payroll, reconciliation, and reporting from one interface
- Natural-language interface is accessible for owners with no accounting background

### Cons

- 25-query monthly cap on lower plans is a genuine friction point for active users
- Advanced AI features (Sales Tax AI, full agent capabilities) are gated behind Plus and Advanced tiers
- Zero value if you are not already on QuickBooks Online — there is no standalone access
- US-centric by design; tax guidance and compliance features are weak for non-US businesses
- Complex multi-entity or high-volume accounting can still expose the platform's ceiling regardless of AI features

## Who Should NOT Use Intuit Assist

- **Businesses not on QuickBooks Online** — Intuit Assist is embedded in QBO. If you run Xero, FreshBooks, Wave, or any other platform, there is nothing here without switching your entire accounting stack.
- **Non-US businesses** — Tax assistance and compliance guidance are built around US regulations. International users will find limited localization and weaker jurisdiction-specific guidance.
- **Simple Start users who query frequently** — The 25-query cap will frustrate anyone treating AI as a daily tool. Either upgrade your plan or the friction will undermine the value proposition.
- **Enterprises needing complex consolidation** — QBO Advanced stretches further than lower tiers, but large enterprises with sophisticated multi-entity or multi-currency consolidation needs will outgrow the platform regardless of how good the AI layer is.

## Verdict

For US small businesses already on QuickBooks Online, enabling Intuit Assist is a straightforward decision — it costs nothing extra and answers questions directly from your own data. The draft-first philosophy is the right call for a tool handling real financial records: it accelerates your workflow without removing you from the approval chain, a concrete advantage over competitors that post autonomously.

The 25-query cap on lower tiers and AI feature-gating behind Plus and Advanced are real considerations when choosing your QBO plan level, not reasons to avoid the platform. If you are evaluating accounting software fresh in 2026, QuickBooks Online with Intuit Assist sets the benchmark for AI-native bookkeeping at the small-business tier. Xero JAX and Zoho Zia are credible alternatives — particularly outside the US — but Intuit's depth of transaction data and tight ecosystem integration give Assist a meaningful edge for domestic small-business use.

## FAQ

### Is Intuit Assist free?

It is included with every QuickBooks Online plan at no additional cost. You pay for QBO (starting approximately $30–38/mo at regular pricing) and Assist comes bundled. Some advanced AI capabilities are only available on Plus and Advanced tiers.

### Does Intuit Assist automatically post transactions?

No. It drafts categorizations and suggested actions for your review before anything is posted. This draft-first approach is a deliberate design choice and one of its strongest differentiators from some competing AI accounting tools.

### What is the AI query limit?

Lower-tier QBO plans include a 25-query-per-month cap on AI interactions. Higher-tier plans have higher or uncapped limits. Verify current limits with Intuit directly, as these may change with plan updates.

### Can I use Intuit Assist without QuickBooks?

No. Intuit Assist is an embedded feature of QuickBooks Online and cannot be accessed as a standalone product or connected to other accounting platforms.

### How does Intuit Assist compare to Xero JAX in 2026?

Both are capable AI accounting assistants. The clearest difference is posture: Intuit Assist drafts for approval; Xero JAX applies changes more autonomously in some workflows. For QuickBooks users, Intuit Assist wins by native data access with no platform switch required. For Xero users, JAX is the natural choice.
