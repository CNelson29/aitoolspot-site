## What is Reclaim.ai?

Reclaim.ai is an AI-powered calendar scheduling assistant that automatically finds and blocks time for your tasks, habits, focus sessions, and meetings — organized around your existing priorities. Instead of manually dragging blocks around your calendar, Reclaim's AI engine continuously rearranges your schedule as new events land, keeping your most important work protected.

Built Google Calendar-first and later expanded to Outlook, Reclaim has grown into a full team scheduling platform. Dropbox acquired Reclaim, which has since continued operating as its own product with an accelerated development roadmap. As of 2026, it remains one of the most polished automatic time-blockers available for knowledge workers.

## Key Features

- **AI Task Scheduling:** Connect your task manager (Asana, Linear, Todoist, ClickUp) and Reclaim automatically finds time for each task based on deadline, priority, and available calendar gaps — then adjusts dynamically when meetings appear.
- **AI Habits:** Set recurring habits like deep work, lunch breaks, or exercise, and Reclaim defends those blocks against meeting creep, rescheduling them automatically when conflicts arise.
- **Smart 1:1 Scheduling:** Finds the optimal recurring time for 1:1 meetings across all attendees' live calendars, adjusting as schedules shift without manual back-and-forth.
- **AI Scheduling Links:** Share availability links that respect buffer times, daily meeting limits, and minimum notice requirements — not just raw open calendar slots.
- **Focus Time Protection:** Reclaim actively blocks focus time and resists meetings that would fragment your deep work sessions.
- **Team OOO Calendar:** Available on the Business plan — a shared view of who is out of office, automatically synced from individual calendar events.
- **Slack Status Sync:** Automatically updates your Slack status based on your calendar, reducing interruptions during focus time or meetings.
- **Integrations:** Zoom, GitHub, Slack, Asana, Linear, Todoist, ClickUp — paid plans unlock the full integration suite.

## Pricing

Reclaim.ai offers four plans as of September 2026. Pricing below reflects monthly billing; Reclaim states yearly billing saves 20%, so verify current annual rates on their website.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Key Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | Free forever | 5 AI Agents, 1-week scheduling range, 1 calendar sync, 1 scheduling link, limited integrations |
| Starter | $10/seat/month | 10 AI Agents per seat, up to 10 seats, 8-week scheduling range, 3 calendar syncs, unlimited integrations |
| Business | $15/seat/month | 100 AI Agents, up to 100 seats, 12-week range, unlimited calendar syncs, team OOO calendar, delegated access |
| Enterprise | $22/seat | Custom features and enterprise support — contact sales |

The free Lite plan is useful for individuals testing the tool, but the 1-week scheduling horizon and single habit slot make it restrictive in daily use. Most knowledge workers who rely on Reclaim seriously will move to Starter at $10/seat/month.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- AI continuously reschedules tasks and habits when meetings appear — not just at initial setup
- Smart 1:1 scheduling eliminates the back-and-forth of recurring meeting coordination
- Scheduling links respect your working preferences, not just open calendar slots
- Deep Google Calendar integration is genuinely seamless
- Slack status auto-sync reduces focus interruptions without manual updates
- Starter plan is competitively priced for the feature set delivered

### Cons

- Google Calendar-first by design; Outlook users get a less complete experience
- No native mobile app — all calendar management is browser-based
- Free tier's 1-week scheduling range is too short for realistic planning workflows
- AI Agent caps on lower tiers limit how many simultaneous tasks or habits you can automate
- Value depends heavily on team-wide adoption — solo use limits the smart scheduling benefits

## Who Should NOT Use Reclaim.ai

- **Outlook-heavy organizations:** Reclaim was architected Google-first. Teams deeply embedded in Microsoft 365 will encounter missing features and friction that erodes the core value.
- **Mobile-first users:** If you primarily manage your schedule from your phone, the browser-only interface is a persistent daily friction point.
- **Teams needing simple booking pages only:** If you only need shareable availability links, purpose-built tools like Calendly or Cal.com are cheaper and simpler.
- **Solo users who won't pay:** The free tier's 1-habit and 1-week-range restrictions make it a tease, not a functional tool for serious time management.

## Verdict

Reclaim.ai earns its position as one of the best AI calendar scheduling tools in 2026. The core promise works: your calendar reorganizes itself around what matters, continuously, without you manually intervening. For Google Calendar teams where members actually adopt it, the productivity return is real.

The Starter plan at $10/seat/month is the practical entry point for most users. The free Lite tier is worth a test-drive, but the 1-week scheduling horizon is a genuine constraint, not just a marketing nudge to upgrade.

If you live in your calendar and constantly lose focus time to reactive scheduling, Reclaim.ai at $10/month is a justifiable expense. If your team is Outlook-first, if you need mobile access, or if you only want a booking page, look at alternatives first.

**Bottom line: Best AI calendar scheduling tool for Google Calendar teams willing to commit to it.**

## FAQ

### Is Reclaim.ai free?

Yes — Reclaim.ai has a free Lite plan. It covers basic AI scheduling but caps you at 5 AI Agents, a 1-week scheduling range, 1 calendar sync, and 1 scheduling link. Most daily users upgrade to Starter at $10/seat/month for the 8-week range and unlimited integrations.

### Does Reclaim.ai work with Outlook?

Reclaim.ai supports Outlook, but it was built Google Calendar-first. Outlook users typically report a less seamless experience compared to Google Calendar users, with some features working differently or not at all.

### Does Reclaim.ai have a mobile app?

As of 2026, Reclaim.ai does not have a native mobile app. All scheduling and configuration is handled through the web interface.

### What did Dropbox acquire from Reclaim.ai?

Dropbox acquired Reclaim.ai outright. Reclaim continues to operate as a standalone product under the Dropbox umbrella with its own development roadmap.

### How is Reclaim.ai different from Calendly?

Calendly focuses on outbound scheduling — sharing a booking page so others can book time with you. Reclaim.ai manages your entire calendar proactively: auto-scheduling tasks, protecting habits, and rearranging blocks when conflicts arise. They solve adjacent but different scheduling problems.
