## What is Duolingo Max?

Duolingo Max is the top subscription tier of Duolingo — the world's most downloaded language-learning app. Launched in 2023, it layers GPT-4-powered AI features on top of everything included in Super Duolingo: ad-free lessons, unlimited hearts, and streak repair. The goal is genuine AI conversation practice inside the app you already use every day.

In 2026, Duolingo Max is available for learners studying Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean (for English speakers). If your target language is outside this list, Max is not an option for your course.

## Key Features

### AI Roleplay

Roleplay places you inside scripted, real-world scenarios — ordering at a restaurant, navigating a job interview, checking into a hotel — and lets you respond via text or voice. GPT-4 evaluates your replies and delivers contextual feedback on word choice, grammar, and fluency. It is the closest Duolingo gets to live conversation practice with a tutor.

### Video Call with Lily

Video Call connects you with Lily, one of Duolingo's AI characters, for open-ended spoken conversation. Unlike the structured Roleplay prompts, these sessions are more free-form and focus on fluency over drill-based accuracy. Note: some 2026 sources indicate Duolingo has been expanding access to this feature beyond Max — check the current app version in your region before upgrading solely for Video Call.

### Explain My Answer (Now Free for Everyone)

As of January 2026, Explain My Answer is no longer locked behind a Max subscription. When you answer a question incorrectly, GPT-4 generates a personalised explanation covering grammar rules, word usage, and context. This was previously the flagship Max benefit; it is now free at every tier. This single change significantly narrows the Max value proposition for 2026.

### Advanced Progress Insights

Max subscribers get deeper analytics on skill gaps, vocabulary retention, and learning velocity compared to Super Duolingo. This helps identify which grammar patterns or word categories need the most repetition.

## Pricing

Duolingo Max pricing varies by region and platform. Based on 2026 sources:

- **Europe:** approximately €14.99/month
- **United States:** approximately $20–30/month
- **Annual plan (US):** approximately $168/year

Duolingo does not publish a single universal price — your App Store or Google Play listing will show the exact rate for your country. Super Duolingo (the tier below Max) runs approximately $7–13/month depending on region, making the Max jump a meaningful additional cost.

**Important 2026 update:** Explain My Answer — previously the headline GPT-4 feature — moved to the free tier in January 2026. Evaluate whether AI Roleplay alone justifies the upgrade cost for your learning goals before subscribing.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Genuine GPT-4 conversation practice without leaving the Duolingo app
- Roleplay scenarios cover practical, high-frequency real-world situations
- Gamification (streaks, leagues, XP) keeps daily study habits strong
- Includes all Super Duolingo perks: no ads, unlimited hearts, streak repair
- No extra setup — works inside the same app already on your phone

### Cons

- Expensive relative to what remains Max-exclusive after January 2026 changes
- Roleplay conversations feel scripted — limited spontaneity compared to open-ended AI chat
- Available for only 7 languages; most Duolingo courses have no Max tier
- Pronunciation feedback is less rigorous than dedicated speech apps
- The most-wanted AI feature (Explain My Answer) is now free — value gap shrank considerably

## Who Should NOT Use Duolingo Max

- **Serious or advanced learners** who need structured grammar instruction or a human teacher. Duolingo Max is supplemental gamified practice, not a full curriculum.
- **Budget-conscious users** — at up to $168/year, alternatives like Babbel or Busuu offer more structured content at lower prices.
- **Learners of unsupported languages** — if you are studying Mandarin, Arabic, Hindi, or any course outside the supported seven, there is no Max tier available to you.
- **Users whose main goal was AI explanations** — Explain My Answer is now free for everyone. You do not need Max to access it.
- **Users prioritising pronunciation accuracy** — dedicated phonetic apps offer more granular scoring than Duolingo Max currently provides.

## Verdict

Duolingo Max in 2026 is a narrower offer than it was at launch. After Explain My Answer moved to the free tier in January 2026, AI Roleplay is the primary remaining Max-exclusive feature worth paying for. It is genuinely useful for practising spoken confidence and real-world vocabulary in context — but at up to $168/year it is a premium you should weigh carefully against your actual usage habits.

If you are already on Super Duolingo and want structured speaking practice inside the same app, the Max upgrade is a reasonable next step. If AI conversation is your primary language-learning goal, standalone tools — or even practising with a general-purpose AI chatbot in your target language — may offer more flexibility at a comparable or lower price point.

**Rating: 7.5/10.** Solid GPT-4 integration in a product millions already use daily. The January 2026 free-tier changes shrink the premium's value, but AI Roleplay remains a legitimate differentiator for committed Duolingo users who want structured speaking practice.

## FAQ

### Is Duolingo Max worth it in 2026?

For committed Duolingo users who want structured AI speaking practice via Roleplay, yes — with reservations. After Explain My Answer became free in January 2026, the value gap between Max and Super Duolingo narrowed significantly. Weigh whether AI Roleplay alone justifies the upgrade cost for your region and study goals.

### What GPT-4 features does Duolingo Max include?

AI Roleplay (scenario-based conversation with GPT-4 feedback) and Video Call with Lily (open-ended spoken AI practice). Explain My Answer — which uses GPT-4 to explain incorrect answers — is now free for all users as of January 2026.

### How much does Duolingo Max cost in 2026?

Approximately €14.99/month in Europe and $20–30/month in the US, with an annual plan around $168/year in the US. Prices vary by region and platform — check your App Store or Google Play listing for the exact local price.

### Which languages support Duolingo Max?

As of 2026: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean — for English-speaking learners. Max features are not available for other language courses on Duolingo.

### What are the best alternatives to Duolingo Max?

Top alternatives in 2026 include Babbel, Busuu, Pimsleur, Rosetta Stone, and Lingvist for structured learning. For AI conversation practice specifically, Copycat Cafe is frequently cited as a direct competitor at a similar price point with more open-ended practice time.
