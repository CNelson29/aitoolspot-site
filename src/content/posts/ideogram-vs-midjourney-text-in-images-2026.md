## What is Ideogram?

Ideogram is an AI image generator built around one thing most tools still get wrong: readable, accurate text inside generated images. Where other generators treat typography as an afterthought, Ideogram treats it as a core output target. The result is a tool that designers, marketers, and social media creators rely on for posters, logos, ads, and any visual where the words inside the image must be correct.

In 2026, Ideogram V3 is the clearest answer to a question every creative professional asks: *Can I put text in an AI image and have it come out right?* On that metric, nothing else comes close.

## Key Features

- **Industry-leading text rendering** — V3 achieves 90-95% text accuracy in generated images, making it reliable for real production work.
- **Typography-first architecture** — Handles multi-word labels, headlines, and logos without the garbled letters that plague other generators.
- **Remix and editing tools** — Inpaint, outpaint, and style-transfer features let you iterate on a generation rather than start from scratch.
- **API access** — Available on Plus and above, letting developers and agencies integrate Ideogram into automated creative pipelines.
- **Free tier** — 10 generations per day at no cost, enough for evaluation and light use.
- **Web-based workflow** — No Discord required; a clean browser interface accessible from any device.

Many professional designers run both: Ideogram for text-critical assets, Midjourney for everything else.
