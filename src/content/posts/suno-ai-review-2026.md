## What is Suno AI?

Suno AI is a text-to-music generator that produces complete, fully produced songs from a short prompt. Enter a genre, mood, or your own lyrics, and Suno returns a track with vocals, instruments, and arrangement — no music production background required.

By February 2026, Suno had crossed 2 million paid subscribers and $300 million in annual recurring revenue, making it the largest AI music platform by subscriber count. The platform has released model v5.5 and has navigated ongoing legal disputes with major labels including Warner over training data practices — a backdrop worth knowing before committing to a workflow built around it.

## Key Features

- **Text-to-song generation** — describe a song in plain language and get a fully produced track in seconds, including vocals.
- **Lyrics mode** — paste your own lyrics and Suno sets them to music in your chosen style and genre.
- **Genre and mood controls** — specify tempo, genre, energy, instrumentation, and vocal character.
- **Audio upload** — all plans, including free, support uploading up to 1 minute of audio as a reference or stem.
- **Editing tools** — post-generation tweaks available on Pro and Premier plans.
- **Model access** — free plan includes v4.5-all; paid plans unlock the best available models including v5.5.

## Pricing

Suno uses a credit system. Each generation costs credits, and **failed generations still deduct credits** on all plans. Credits do not roll over — the only exception is add-on credits purchased separately.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Credits | Commercial use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | 50/day (~10 songs), renews daily | No |
| Pro | $10/mo | $8/mo | ~500/month | Yes |
| Premier | $30/mo | $24/mo | ~2,000/month | Yes |

The free plan's 50 daily credits expire at midnight regardless of whether you use them. Pro and Premier monthly credits also expire at the end of each billing cycle. Annual billing saves roughly 20% over monthly billing — $8 vs $10 on Pro, $24 vs $30 on Premier.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Free plan is genuinely usable — 50 credits per day is about 10 songs
- Full song output with vocals and arrangement, not just instrumentals
- Lyrics upload lets songwriters hear their words set to music instantly
- Commercial rights included on all paid plans
- Fast output compared to alternatives
- Audio upload available even on the free tier

### Cons

- Credits never roll over on any plan — unused credits are lost
- Failed generations still cost credits
- Free plan output cannot be used commercially
- Monthly billing costs 25% more than annual billing
- Shared generation queue on free plan means slower output during peak hours
- Ongoing copyright litigation creates uncertainty around long-term licensing stability

## Who Should NOT Use Suno AI

- **Producers who need stems** — Suno outputs a mixed audio file, not separated tracks suitable for professional mastering.
- **Businesses wanting commercial use at no cost** — the free plan explicitly prohibits commercial use; the minimum for monetization is $8/month on annual billing.
- **Light or sporadic users on paid plans** — monthly credits expire at cycle end, so users who generate infrequently may pay for credits they never use.
- **Anyone requiring legal certainty** — active litigation with major labels means the licensing landscape could shift; factor that in before building a commercial workflow around Suno output.

## Verdict

Suno AI is the most accessible AI music generator available in 2026. The free tier — 50 daily credits, v4.5-all model access, audio upload — is a meaningful starting point without spending anything. The jump to Pro at $8/month (annual) unlocks commercial rights and editing tools, which is a reasonable price for content creators, podcast producers, and indie filmmakers who need original music regularly.

The main structural frustrations are the expiring credits and the cost of failed generations. Neither is a dealbreaker, but they reward users who generate in deliberate batches rather than casual one-offs. For heavier output, Premier at $24/month (annual) makes more financial sense than Pro once you factor in the credit math.

The ongoing legal situation with major labels is worth monitoring, but it has not disrupted service or changed the licensing terms for paying users as of September 2026.

**Rating: 8.4 / 10** — The leading consumer AI music tool, with pricing that rewards annual commitment and a free plan that actually works.

## FAQ

### Is Suno AI free?

Yes. The free plan provides 50 credits per day (approximately 10 songs), renewed daily. Free credits do not carry over, and free-tier output cannot be used commercially.

### How much does Suno AI cost in 2026?

Pro is $8/month on annual billing or $10/month on monthly billing. Premier is $24/month on annual billing or $30/month on monthly billing.

### Do Suno credits roll over?

No. Free daily credits expire each day. Pro and Premier monthly credits also expire at cycle end. Only separately purchased add-on credits carry forward.

### Can I use Suno music commercially?

Not on the free plan. Pro and Premier plans include commercial use rights.

### What happens if a generation fails?

Failed generations still deduct credits from your balance on all plans.

### What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

Annual billing costs about 20% less: Pro drops from $10 to $8/month, Premier from $30 to $24/month. The Suno FAQ historically cited the monthly-billing figures ($10/$30), which caused some confusion — both rates are correct depending on how you pay.
