## Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai for Meeting Notes in 2026

Both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai drop a bot into your calls and hand back a transcript — but they optimize for fundamentally different things. After independent head-to-head testing and multiple 2026 reviews, the short answer is: **Otter leads on transcription accuracy, Fireflies leads on integrations and meeting automation.** The right pick depends on where your notes need to go after the meeting ends.

## Transcription Accuracy

Otter.ai is consistently cited as the accuracy leader in 2026 meeting AI comparisons, with multiple sources noting approximately 95% transcription accuracy. It shines in live settings — the transcript appears in real time, making it useful for participants who want to follow along or catch corrections mid-meeting.

An independent August 2026 test by Context Rankings ran both tools across four identical meetings. Otter scored 82/100 overall; Fireflies scored 85/100 — winning three of the four rounds. That result reflects Fireflies' edge in note structure, action-item extraction, and post-meeting workflow output, even where Otter's raw word-for-word transcript fidelity is higher.

## Integrations and Workflow

Fireflies.ai was built around meeting automation and a wide integration surface. It pipes summaries and action items into CRMs, project tools, and communication platforms more reliably than Otter. Teams using Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, or Slack will find Fireflies fits more naturally into the post-meeting workflow without manual copy-paste.

Otter.ai is strongest as a live transcript and searchable conversation workspace. Its integrations exist but are narrower. If your team primarily wants an accurate, searchable record of what was said — rather than automated CRM updates — Otter's simpler surface is an advantage.

## Pricing

Both tools offer free tiers. Paid plans across the meeting AI tools market in 2026 range from free to approximately $59/month depending on seats, storage, and feature tier. Both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai sit within this range on their paid plans — check each tool's current pricing page directly, as meeting AI pricing shifted notably through 2025–2026.

**Free tier caveats:** Otter's free plan limits monthly transcription minutes. Fireflies' free plan limits storage. Neither free tier is suitable for teams with high meeting volume.

## Pros and Cons

- **Pro:** Industry-leading transcription accuracy (~95%)
- **Pro:** Real-time live transcription during calls
- **Pro:** Clean, searchable conversation workspace
- **Pro:** Calendar-based auto-join
- **Con:** Fewer integrations than Fireflies
- **Con:** Meeting analytics are basic
- **Con:** Weaker post-meeting automation

### Fireflies.ai

- **Pro:** Advanced meeting analytics and cross-meeting search
- **Pro:** Strong action-item and follow-up extraction
- **Pro:** Wins on overall workflow score in independent 2026 testing

- **Con:** Raw transcript accuracy slightly behind Otter

## Who Should NOT Use Either Tool

If your meetings regularly switch between two or more languages, both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai underperform. Neither tool handles multilingual meetings reliably in 2026 — transcript quality degrades when speakers shift languages mid-call.

Teams already living inside Notion may be better served by Notion AI Meeting Notes, which is native rather than a separate bot. High-volume sales teams may find tl;dv — built specifically for sales workflows — a stronger fit than either tool.

## Verdict

**Choose Otter.ai if** you prioritize word-for-word accuracy, need live transcription during calls, or want a simple searchable archive of conversations without complex integration requirements.

**Choose Fireflies.ai if** you need robust integrations (especially CRM), want advanced meeting analytics, or run a team where the value of a meeting lives in what happens after it ends — follow-ups, CRM entries, task creation.

For most teams evaluating both in 2026, Fireflies edges out Otter in overall workflow value. But Otter remains the better pure transcription tool. Neither is ideal if multilingual meetings are a regular scenario.

## FAQ

### Is Otter.ai more accurate than Fireflies.ai?

Otter.ai is cited as the accuracy leader at approximately 95% in 2026 comparisons. However, in a head-to-head August 2026 test by Context Rankings, Fireflies scored higher overall (85/100 vs 82/100) due to stronger note structure and post-meeting automation.

### Which has the better free plan?

Both offer free tiers with meaningful limits on minutes or storage. For the most generous free tier in this category, Fathom is frequently cited as the leader in 2026 meeting AI comparisons.

### Does Fireflies.ai integrate with Salesforce?

Yes. Fireflies.ai is widely recognized for extensive CRM integrations including Salesforce and HubSpot — a clear differentiator over Otter.ai for sales and revenue teams.

### Can either tool handle meetings in multiple languages?

Not reliably. Both Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai struggle with multilingual meetings in 2026. If speakers switch languages mid-call, transcription quality drops significantly for both tools.

### Which tool is better for remote teams?

Fireflies.ai is generally the stronger choice for distributed teams that rely on CRM workflows and async meeting reviews. Otter.ai suits teams that value live accuracy and a searchable conversation record above integration depth.
