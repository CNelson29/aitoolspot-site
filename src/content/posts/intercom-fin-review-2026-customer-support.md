## What is Intercom Fin?

Intercom Fin is an AI customer service agent built on Intercom's proprietary Fin AI Engine. In May 2026, Intercom rebranded the entire platform under the *Fin* name, reflecting how central autonomous AI resolution has become to their product. Fin handles customer queries across live chat, email, in-app messaging, and voice — all routed through a unified inbox and backed by your existing knowledge base.

Unlike scripted chatbots, Fin uses large language models to understand context and resolve complex tickets without human intervention. It is the most deeply integrated AI support option for teams already running on Intercom, and consistently ranks as the highest-resolution-rate pre-built agent on the market heading into 2026.

## Key Features

- **Omnichannel coverage:** Live chat, email, in-app messaging, and Fin Voice — managed from one workspace.
- **Proprietary Fin AI Engine:** Designed to resolve complex queries, not just deflect simple ones. Repeatedly cited as best-in-class for resolution rates among pre-built agents.
- **Knowledge base integration:** Ingests your existing help docs, FAQs, and articles with no manual scripting required.
- **Intelligent human handoff:** Escalates to a human agent when confidence is low or the customer requests it, with full context carried over.
- **Multi-language support:** Responds automatically in the customer's language.
- **Fin Copilot:** An AI assistant for your human agents that suggests replies, summarizes conversations, and surfaces relevant articles mid-conversation.
- **Resolution analytics:** Built-in dashboards tracking resolution rates, deflection percentages, and cost per ticket so you can optimize over time.

## Pricing

Intercom (now Fin) uses a seat-based model with an additional per-resolution fee for the AI agent. As of 2026, the plans are:

- **Essential:** $29/seat/month (annual) or $39/seat/month (monthly)
- **Advanced:** $85/seat/month (annual) or $99/seat/month (monthly)
- **Expert:** $132/seat/month (annual) or $139/seat/month (monthly)

All plans include Fin AI, but each AI-resolved conversation costs an additional **$0.99**. That fee only applies when Fin actually resolves the issue — unresolved conversations handed to humans are not charged. A free trial is available, and base plans start around $74/month before AI usage fees.

The economic breakpoint matters: independent testing found that teams below roughly 300 AI-resolved conversations per month will often pay less with flat-rate alternatives like Tidio Lyro. Above that threshold — especially with solid documentation — the per-resolution model becomes cost-effective because you only pay for outcomes. That said, multiple user reviews flag that bills can become unpredictable at scale, particularly when Fin's resolution rate underperforms due to gaps in the knowledge base.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Best published AI resolution rates among pre-built customer support agents in 2026
- Broadest channel coverage: chat, email, in-app, voice
- Per-resolution pricing means you pay for value, not just access
- Deep integration with Intercom's human agent workspace and existing workflows
- No scripting needed — Fin learns from your existing help content
- Fin Copilot adds real value for human agents, not just AI-only workflows
- Strong built-in analytics and reporting

### Cons

- $0.99/resolution fee creates unpredictable monthly bills at high volume
- Expensive for low-volume teams or businesses with thin documentation
- Fin Voice setup has been flagged as complex in user reviews
- AI resolution quality drops significantly when the knowledge base is incomplete
- Seat costs plus resolution fees can exceed competitor all-in pricing quickly
- High-volume, low-ARPU use cases (gaming, crypto support) can face disproportionate costs

## Who Should NOT Use Intercom Fin

- **Low-volume support teams** handling fewer than 300 AI-resolved conversations per month — flat-rate tools will cost less and be simpler to budget.
- **Startups with sparse documentation** — Fin's resolution quality is directly tied to your knowledge base. Thin docs mean low resolution rates and $0.99 charges for partial or failed attempts.
- **High-volume, low-ARPU businesses** (gaming platforms, crypto support, free-tier SaaS) — at scale, the per-resolution fee can erase the savings from deflection.
- **Teams not already on Intercom** — migrating your entire support stack just to access Fin is a large commitment. Evaluate total migration cost before deciding.

## Verdict

Intercom Fin is the most capable pre-built AI customer support agent available in 2026. If your team is already on Intercom, handles substantial ticket volume, and maintains solid help documentation, Fin will meaningfully reduce human support load and likely justify its cost. The per-resolution model is fair in principle — you pay when the AI delivers — but it introduces billing volatility that has surprised many teams.

For mid-market and enterprise operations with mature support content, Fin is the clear top choice. For smaller teams or businesses with documentation gaps, run the numbers on your monthly volume before committing. The quality is real; so is the cost.

## FAQ

### Is Intercom Fin free?

No. Fin requires a paid Intercom seat plan starting at $29/seat/month (annual billing), plus $0.99 per resolved conversation for Fin AI usage.

### How does the $0.99/resolution fee work?

You are charged $0.99 each time Fin AI closes a conversation without human intervention. Conversations escalated to a human agent do not incur the resolution fee.

### What is the difference between Intercom and Fin?

In May 2026, Intercom rebranded the entire platform as Fin. The AI customer service agent is Fin; the broader platform (formerly Intercom) is now built around it. Same product, new name and positioning.

### Who are Intercom Fin's main competitors in 2026?

The main alternatives include Tidio Lyro (better value for low-volume teams), Zendesk AI, HubSpot Breeze, and Helply — each with different pricing models and integration ecosystems.

### Does Fin replace a human support team entirely?

No. Fin is designed as a hybrid system. It handles a large share of queries autonomously, but complex, sensitive, or ambiguous issues escalate to human agents. Think of it as a very capable first line of defense, not a full team replacement.
