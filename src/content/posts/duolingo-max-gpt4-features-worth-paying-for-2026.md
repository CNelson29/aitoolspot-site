## What is Duolingo Max?

Duolingo Max is the top-tier subscription of Duolingo, the world's most-downloaded language-learning app. It adds GPT-4-powered AI conversation features on top of everything included in Super Duolingo. In 2026, Max remains the only way to access Duolingo's AI speaking tools — but the value equation shifted after one of its headline features was made free for everyone.

## Key Features

### Video Call with Lily

The most distinctive Max-exclusive feature is Video Call — a real-time spoken conversation with Lily, an AI character powered by GPT-4. You speak, Lily responds dynamically, and the system flags mistakes in context. For learners with speaking anxiety, this low-stakes format is genuinely useful: there's no human on the other end to judge you, but the conversation adapts to what you actually say.

### Roleplay

Roleplay places you in scenario-based speaking exercises — ordering food, navigating a hotel check-in, handling a market negotiation. GPT-4 adjusts to your answers rather than following a fixed script, which makes it more demanding and more realistic than traditional dialogue exercises. Intermediate learners who already have a vocabulary base get the most out of this feature.

### Explain My Answer — Now Free

As of January 2026, Explain My Answer — which provides grammar breakdowns after each exercise — is available to all Duolingo users at no cost. It was previously a Max-exclusive. This is a significant change: one of the three headline AI features no longer requires a Max subscription. If grammar explanations were your main reason to upgrade, the free tier now covers that need.

### Everything in Super Duolingo

Max includes the full Super feature set: unlimited hearts, no ads, offline lessons, streak repair, and streak freeze. These quality-of-life improvements are genuinely useful for daily learners but are not AI-differentiated — Super Duolingo provides them at a lower price.

## Language Availability

As of mid-2026, the GPT-4 AI features in Max are available for Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean — for English speakers. If your target language is not on this list, upgrading to Max provides no AI benefit over Super Duolingo.

## Pricing

Duolingo Max costs approximately $30/month or $168/year (billed annually). The annual plan represents a significant discount over monthly billing. Duolingo does not publish exact pricing on its public website — figures are based on widely reported user data and third-party reviews current as of early 2026; check the Duolingo app directly for your region's current rate, as pricing varies by country.

For reference, Super Duolingo is available at a substantially lower price point. Max is roughly double to triple the Super price depending on region. That gap is the core of the upgrade decision.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- GPT-4 powers genuinely adaptive conversations, not rigid scripted chatbots
- Video Call with Lily provides low-pressure speaking practice with real-time feedback
- Roleplay scenarios go beyond standard Duolingo exercises for intermediate learners
- Includes all Super Duolingo perks (no ads, unlimited hearts, offline access)
- Proven gamification model keeps daily habit formation strong

### Cons

- Explain My Answer is now free — one less reason to pay the Max premium
- AI features limited to 7 languages; learners of other languages get no AI benefit
- $30/month is expensive for casual or infrequent users
- Gamification-first design means AI features feel like add-ons rather than the core experience
- Competitors such as Babbel, Busuu, and PolyChat offer structured or unlimited speaking practice at different price points

## Who Should NOT Use Duolingo Max

- **Beginners:** The AI conversation features assume you can already construct basic sentences. Absolute beginners benefit more from free Duolingo or Super before considering Max.
- **Learners of unsupported languages:** If you are studying Mandarin, Arabic, Hindi, or any language outside the current seven, the Max AI features simply do not apply.
- **Casual five-minutes-a-day users:** The premium is hard to justify if you are not regularly engaging with the speaking features. Super Duolingo is more cost-effective for light users.
- **Grammar-explanation seekers:** Since Explain My Answer is now free for all users, there is no need to pay for Max to access grammar breakdowns.

## Verdict

Duolingo Max makes a strong case for one specific type of learner: an intermediate speaker studying a supported language who wants regular, low-pressure AI conversation practice within an app they already use daily. Video Call and Roleplay are not marketing fluff — GPT-4 makes the interactions feel genuinely adaptive, and the absence of judgment from a human partner removes a real barrier for many learners.

That said, the value proposition narrowed in 2026. Explain My Answer going free removed one of three AI differentiators. At $30/month, you are now essentially paying for speaking practice alone. If that is your specific bottleneck, Max earns its price. If you want grammar support, the free tier now covers it. If you want intensive unlimited conversation AI, dedicated alternatives may offer more practice time for a similar investment.

**Bottom line:** Recommended for committed intermediate learners in supported languages. Skip it if you are a beginner, a casual user, or studying an unsupported language.

## FAQ

### Is Duolingo Max worth it in 2026?

Yes, if you are an intermediate learner studying Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, or Korean and want AI-driven speaking practice within the Duolingo ecosystem. No, if you are a beginner, a casual user, or studying an unsupported language — the free tier or Super Duolingo will serve you better.

### What GPT-4 features does Duolingo Max include?

Two Max-exclusive features: Video Call (real-time AI conversation with the character Lily) and Roleplay (scenario-based speaking practice). Explain My Answer, which provides grammar breakdowns, became free for all Duolingo users in January 2026.

### How much does Duolingo Max cost?

Approximately $30/month or $168/year as of 2026. Duolingo does not publish pricing publicly, so check the app for the current rate in your region.

### What languages support Duolingo Max AI features?

Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean for English speakers, as of mid-2026. AI conversation features are not available for other language courses.

### What are the best Duolingo Max alternatives?

Babbel and Busuu offer structured courses with their own conversation tools. PolyChat is noted for intensive AI conversation practice without daily limits. The right alternative depends on whether you prioritize gamification, structure, or raw conversation volume.
