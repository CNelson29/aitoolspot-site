## What is Harvey?

Harvey is an enterprise-grade AI platform built specifically for law firms and legal teams. Backed by over $300 million in funding and valued at around $11 billion as of early 2026, it has become the AI tool of choice for a majority of the Am Law 100. More than 1,000 organizations across 60 countries use it, with high-profile clients including Allen & Overy and PwC's legal division.

Harvey layers frontier AI models over a firm's own data, enabling legal research, contract drafting, due diligence, and billing workflow support — including time entry automation. For a large firm with complex billing requirements and a dedicated IT team, it represents the current state of the art in legal AI. For a small law firm, the calculus is very different.

## Key Features

- **Legal research & drafting:** Summarizes case law, drafts pleadings, and assists with contract review using jurisdiction-aware reasoning.
- **Due diligence automation:** Processes large document sets for M&A and litigation support at scale — the flagship use case for big-firm clients.
- **Custom workflows:** Harvey supports over 18,000 custom workflows, giving enterprise clients deep configurability across practice areas.
- **Billing & time entry support:** Assists attorneys in reconstructing and drafting time entries from work records, reducing write-offs on complex matters.
- **Enterprise governance:** Built-in audit trails, data security certifications, and compliance controls designed for regulated legal environments.
- **Integrations:** Connects with document management systems and practice management platforms commonly used at large firms.

## Pricing

Harvey does not publish pricing publicly. It operates on a custom enterprise contract model — firms must contact the sales team for a quote. According to The Crossing Report (April 2026), small firms that go through the demo process consistently describe sticker shock when the number arrives. The Litmus review (July 2026) scores Harvey's Value category at 7.0 out of 10 — its lowest individual score — reflecting that the price-to-value ratio is hardest to justify outside large enterprise deployments.

If budget transparency matters for your firm's decision-making process, Harvey is not the right tool at this stage. Competitors like Clio Duo, Spellbook, and CoCounsel publish tiered pricing designed for practices of varying sizes.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Best-in-class AI reasoning for complex legal tasks
- Extremely broad feature set with 18,000+ custom workflows
- Strong enterprise governance, data security, and compliance controls
- Dominant Am Law 100 adoption signals proven reliability at scale
- Dedicated support and onboarding for enterprise clients

### Cons

- No published pricing — enterprise quotes only, with reported sticker shock for small firms
- Designed for large-firm scale; small firms may never use the majority of features
- Sales-led onboarding process is slow for firms that need to move fast
- Likely requires IT resources most small firms do not have in-house
- Better-fitted, more affordable alternatives exist for practices under 20 attorneys

## Who Should NOT Use Harvey

Harvey is the wrong tool if you run a firm with fewer than 20 attorneys, operate with a constrained tech budget, or need pricing clarity before committing. The platform is engineered for high-volume, complex enterprise workflows — the throughput an Am Law 50 firm generates. A solo practitioner or a boutique firm handling routine matters will pay for capabilities they will never touch.

As The Legal Prompts (2026) puts it bluntly: Harvey is the strongest enterprise legal AI platform on the market — and the wrong purchase for most firms reading reviews of it. Better-fit alternatives for small law firms include:

- **Clio Duo** — AI built directly into Clio's practice management suite, with pricing already familiar to small firms.
- **Spellbook** — Contract drafting and review focused, with published plans scaled to smaller teams.
- **CoCounsel (Thomson Reuters)** — Legal research and drafting with tiered pricing that matches firm size.
- **Legora** — Positioned explicitly as a Harvey alternative with a more accessible entry point for mid-market and smaller practices.

## Verdict

Harvey is arguably the most capable AI platform in legal right now. Litmus rates it 86/100 overall, and its dominance among Am Law 100 firms is well-documented. If you are at a large firm evaluating enterprise legal AI, it deserves serious consideration.

For small law firms evaluating Harvey for billing and daily legal work, the honest answer is simpler: Harvey is not built for you, and it does not pretend to be. The custom enterprise pricing, sales-led onboarding, and feature set engineered around high-volume big-firm workflows make it overkill — and likely out of reach — for a 5- or 10-attorney practice. The market has better-fitting, more affordable options. Save Harvey for when your headcount and budget warrant it.

**Rating for small law firms: 6.5/10** — exceptional technology, wrong fit for the segment.

## FAQ

### Does Harvey AI offer a free trial?

Harvey does not publicly advertise a free trial. Firms typically go through a sales-led demo process before gaining access. This alone is a significant barrier for small firms that need to evaluate tools quickly before committing budget.

### Is Harvey AI good for solo attorneys or small boutique firms?

Based on available reporting, Harvey targets large enterprise legal teams and is not optimized for small practices. Solo attorneys and boutique firms are better served by tools like Clio Duo or Spellbook, which are priced and designed for smaller-scale workflows.

### What does Harvey AI cost per user?

Harvey does not publish per-user or per-seat pricing. All pricing is negotiated through their enterprise sales process. Small firms that have gone through that process report the figures are not budget-friendly for smaller operations.

### How does Harvey handle legal billing specifically?

Harvey assists with time entry drafting and reconstruction from work records, helping attorneys capture billable time more accurately and reduce write-offs. This is a genuinely useful feature — but it is bundled inside a much larger enterprise platform with a price tag to match.

### What are the best Harvey alternatives for small law firms?

The most commonly cited alternatives for small firms are Clio Duo (integrated practice management AI with transparent pricing), Spellbook (contract drafting with small-firm plans), CoCounsel by Thomson Reuters (legal research and drafting), and Legora (general legal AI with a more accessible entry point).
