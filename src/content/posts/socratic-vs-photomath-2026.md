## What is Socratic by Google?

Socratic is Google's free homework-help app for K-12 students. Point your phone camera at any question — math, science, history, English — and the app uses Google's AI models layered over web search results to surface step-by-step explanations, curated videos, and definitions. No account required, no subscription, no paywall.

Photomath, also now under Google's umbrella (acquired in 2022), takes a narrower but deeper approach: it focuses exclusively on math, from arithmetic through calculus, and generates its own step-by-step solutions rather than pulling from web results.

## Key Features

### Socratic by Google

- **Multi-subject coverage** — math, science, social studies, and English literature all supported
- **Camera and voice input** — snap a photo of a question or ask verbally
- **Google Search overlay** — explanations sourced from trusted web results, Khan Academy videos, and Wikipedia
- **No account needed** — open and use immediately
- **Completely free** — no subscription tier, no ads

### Photomath

- **Math-only depth** — arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, and calculus with multiple solution methods
- **Camera scanning** — reads both handwritten and printed equations
- **Native step-by-step engine** — solutions generated internally, not pulled from web search
- **Animated tutorials (Plus)** — visual walkthroughs for harder concepts
- **Textbook-specific solutions (Plus)** — matches answers to your exact edition

## Pricing

### Socratic by Google

Completely free. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. Available on iOS and Android.

### Photomath

The core step-by-step math solver is free with no ads and no time limits, covering most K-12 and introductory college math. **Photomath Plus** adds animated tutorials and textbook-specific solutions for **$9.99/month** or **$69.99/year**, with a 7-day free trial to evaluate whether the upgrade suits your needs.

## Pros and Cons

### Socratic by Google — Pros

- 100% free with no hidden tiers or upsells
- Covers every major K-12 subject, not just math
- Backed by Google's search index for broad, reliable sourcing
- Great for understanding the conceptual "why" behind an answer

### Socratic by Google — Cons

- Math explanations are shallower than Photomath's for complex problems
- Answer quality depends on the quality of underlying web results
- No animated tutorials or interactive practice mode
- Less effective for advanced math (pre-calculus and beyond)

## Who Should NOT Use Socratic

If your primary need is deep, step-by-step math solving — especially for algebra, trigonometry, or calculus — Photomath's native engine will outperform Socratic in that specific lane. Socratic is also not the right pick if you want textbook-matched solutions or animated visual explanations, both of which require Photomath Plus.

College-level STEM students who need rigorous, multi-method solution paths will likely find tools like Wolfram Alpha or Photomath Plus more suitable for their level.

## Verdict

**Socratic by Google** wins on breadth and price. It is the best free, all-subjects homework app for K-12 students who need help across multiple subjects without spending anything. Its Google-backed sourcing makes explanations reliable enough for the vast majority of assignments.

**Photomath** wins on math depth. If your problem is a math equation and you want the cleanest, most detailed step-by-step breakdown, Photomath's free tier is more focused and its Plus tier adds clear value for heavy math users.

**Bottom line:** Use both. Socratic for science, history, and English; Photomath for math. Neither costs money at the free tier, and together they cover nearly every K-12 homework scenario — making this a genuinely free, full-coverage study stack.

## FAQ

### Is Socratic by Google still free in 2026?

Yes. Socratic remains completely free with no subscription plans of any kind.

### Does Photomath cost money?

The core math solver is free with no ads. Photomath Plus costs $9.99/month or $69.99/year and adds animated tutorials and textbook-specific solutions, with a 7-day free trial.

### Which is better for high school math?

Photomath offers deeper, natively generated step-by-step explanations for math-heavy assignments. For a student who needs help across all subjects, using both apps together is the optimal strategy.

### Can Socratic help with subjects other than math?

Yes — Socratic covers science, social studies, and English literature in addition to math, making it significantly more versatile than Photomath for general K-12 homework help.

### Who owns Photomath and Socratic?

Both are under Google's ownership. Google developed Socratic directly and acquired Photomath in 2022. They remain separate apps with distinct approaches.
