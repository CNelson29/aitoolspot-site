## What is Opus Clip?

Opus Clip is an AI-powered video repurposing tool that automatically extracts short clips from long-form content. Upload a podcast, webinar, or YouTube video and the platform identifies the strongest moments, adds animated captions, reframes for vertical, and exports ready-to-post clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

In 2026 it remains the most widely recognized name in AI clip extraction — and for a specific creator profile, it earns that reputation. For everyone else, the credit model creates real friction worth understanding before you subscribe.

## Key Features

- **AI Clip Extraction** — Analyzes transcripts and engagement signals to surface the highest-potential moments from your source video automatically.
- **Virality Score** — Each extracted clip receives a predicted social performance score, helping you prioritize which to post first.
- **Auto Captions** — Styled, animated captions are generated and burned in with solid accuracy across English and other supported languages.
- **AI B-Roll** — Available on Pro: 50 AI-generated B-roll clips per day plus unlimited stock footage to fill visual gaps.
- **Social Scheduling** — Pro plan supports direct publishing and scheduling across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and X.
- **4K Export & Brand Kit** — Watermark-free 4K output and brand colors, fonts, and logo on every clip from Starter upward.
- **Bulk Export & XML Export** — Pro users can export clips in bulk and generate Adobe Premiere XML timelines for further editing.

## Pricing

Opus Clip uses a credit system where **1 credit = 1 minute of source video processed**. As of 2026, the four tiers are:

| Plan | Price | Credits | Notable Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0/mo | 60 min/mo | Watermark, 1080p, files expire in 3 days |
| Starter | $15/mo | 150 min/mo | No watermark, 4K, Brand Kit |
| Pro | $29/mo ($14.50/mo annual) | 300 min/mo | AI B-Roll, social scheduling, bulk export, XML export to Premiere |
| Business | Custom | Custom | API access, team features, negotiable rates |

**The credit trap:** Because credits are consumed per source minute — not per clip — a single 60-minute podcast costs 60 credits. That is 40% of your entire Starter allowance in one upload. Solo creators on monthly Starter plans frequently hit zero mid-month. If you produce long-form content regularly, do the math before subscribing: multiply your average video length by the number of videos you process monthly and compare against the plan limits.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- AI clip detection is genuinely accurate — surfaces hooks that manual review misses
- Virality scoring adds a useful prioritization layer without extra effort
- Pro plan covers the full workflow: clipping, B-roll, captions, scheduling, and Premiere XML
- Free tier is functional enough to evaluate quality before paying
- 4K watermark-free output from $15/month

### Cons

- Per-source-minute credit model is expensive for long-form heavy users
- Free files expire after 3 days — time pressure on the trial
- Business pricing is opaque; requires a custom sales conversation
- Competitors like Ssemble charge per clip (not per source minute), which can be 3–4× cheaper for longer content

## Who Should NOT Use Opus Clip

- **Sporadic creators** who publish once or twice a month — credits evaporate on a single long upload, making the per-credit cost high relative to output
- **Budget-first creators** — CapCut is free with manual editing, Ssemble charges per clip rather than per source minute, and Vizard offers competitive team collaboration pricing
- **Teams needing built-in collaboration** — Business tier requires custom negotiation; Vizard and Ssemble may be simpler to procure for mid-size teams
- **Short-form native creators** who shoot vertical content directly and have no long-form source material to repurpose

## Verdict

Opus Clip is the right tool for one profile: the consistent long-form creator — weekly podcaster, regular webinar host, YouTube channel pushing 30–60 minute episodes — whose primary bottleneck is clip extraction time. For that creator, the Pro plan at $29/month (or roughly $14.50/month billed annually) delivers a near-complete workflow and pays for itself quickly in saved editing hours.

Outside that profile, the per-source-minute credit model creates real cost pressure and alternatives are worth evaluating. Calculate your monthly processing minutes honestly before committing.

## FAQ

### Does Opus Clip have a free plan?

Yes. The free tier provides 60 processing minutes per month with watermarked 1080p output. Files expire after 3 days, so export your clips immediately after processing.

### How does the credit system work?

One credit equals one minute of source video. A 60-minute podcast costs 60 credits regardless of how many clips you extract from it. Starter includes 150 credits/month; Pro includes 300.

### What are the best Opus Clip alternatives in 2026?

Ssemble charges per clip rather than per source minute, making it 3–4× cheaper for long-form content. CapCut is free with manual editing. Vizard supports team collaboration with a more transparent pricing structure.

### Is Opus Clip worth it for solo creators?

It depends on publishing cadence. If you produce long-form content consistently and clip extraction is your biggest time bottleneck, Pro at $29/month is defensible. If you post sporadically or process short videos, a per-clip competitor or the free tier may be more economical.

### Does Pro include social media scheduling?

Yes. The Pro plan includes direct scheduling to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and X, plus AI B-roll generation, bulk export, and XML export to Adobe Premiere.
