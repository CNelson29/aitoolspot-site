## What Are Free AI Video Generators?

Free AI video generators turn a text prompt or still image into a moving video clip — no camera, no crew, no editing skills required. By mid-2026, roughly sixty tools market themselves as AI video generators, but independent testing across multiple 2026 roundups narrows the genuinely useful free options to around eight to eleven tools worth your time.

This guide covers what actually matters to anyone ready to pick a tool: real export quality, honest watermark policies, actual credit limits, and which free tiers survive real use without forcing an immediate upgrade.

## Key Features to Look For

- **No watermark on the free tier** — Some tools brand every free export. Hailuo AI is the standout: multiple 2026 reviewers confirm it ships watermark-free video on its free plan.
- **Clip length and daily limits** — Free tiers typically cap clips at 5–10 seconds per generation, with daily or monthly generation limits that vary by tool.
- **Image-to-video (I2V)** — Animate a still image into a moving clip. Reviewers in 2026 rate Hailuo AI's I2V as competitive with paid models.
- **Text-to-video (T2V)** — Generate a clip directly from a written prompt. Veo 3 (Google), Kling, Runway, and Pika all support this mode.
- **Resolution and aspect ratio** — Look for 720p or 1080p free exports and 9:16 support if you are creating Shorts or Reels.
- **Low friction signup** — Some tools require a paid account just to test them. The best free tiers let you generate before you commit.
