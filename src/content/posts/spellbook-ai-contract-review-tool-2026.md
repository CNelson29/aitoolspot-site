## What is Spellbook?

Spellbook is an AI-powered contract drafting and review tool built as a Microsoft Word add-in. Founded in 2022 and headquartered in Toronto, it has grown into one of the leading legal AI platforms for transactional lawyers and in-house contract teams who already work inside the Microsoft 365 ecosystem. The core philosophy is simple: meet attorneys where they are, rather than forcing them into a new platform.

The product runs on OpenAI's GPT-4 family with additional models from Anthropic, layered with fine-tuning and prompting trained specifically on contract data. In April 2026, Spellbook closed a $50M Series B at a $350M valuation and signed a two-year exclusive partnership with the Canadian Bar Association, covering roughly 40,000 Canadian lawyers, judges, notaries, and law students — a strong signal of institutional credibility.

## Key Features

- **AI Redlines in Tracked Changes:** Spellbook generates precise redlines directly inside Microsoft Word using native tracked-changes format, so edits look and behave exactly like attorney-made edits — no copy-pasting, no formatting loss.
- **Custom Playbooks:** Save your firm's negotiating standards once, and Spellbook applies them automatically on every review, flagging any deviation from your preferred positions.
- **Clause Benchmarking:** Compare contract clauses against market data from over 2,300 contract types to identify whether terms are standard, aggressive, or unusual for the deal type.
- **Risk Detection:** Automatically flags non-standard terms, missing clauses, and high-risk provisions across supplier MSAs, vendor agreements, and commercial contracts — including multi-country reviews.
- **Clause Library:** A built-in library of pre-approved language that persists across documents, eliminating repetitive drafting work session after session.
- **Deal Search:** Search across every signed contract in your portfolio to surface precedents, obligations, and relevant language at scale.

## Pricing

Spellbook does not publish pricing publicly — a demo is required for a formal quote. Based on third-party review sources, the Associate plan starts at approximately $89 per user per month billed annually, with Pro and Enterprise tiers available at higher price points. Reviewer estimates place the full range between roughly $99 and $350 per user per month depending on tier and firm size.

A 7-day free trial is available at [spellbook.com](https://spellbook.com/) without requiring a sales call first. Enterprise pricing requires a demo.

*Important: the figures above come from third-party review sources, not Spellbook's own published rate card. Confirm current pricing directly with Spellbook before making a purchase decision.*

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Lives entirely inside Microsoft Word — zero workflow disruption for attorneys
- Persistent playbooks that remember your firm's negotiating standards across every review
- Clause benchmarking against 2,300+ contract types provides real market context
- Tracked-changes redlines look and feel like attorney edits, not AI output
- 7-day free trial available without a sales call
- Strong institutional backing: $50M Series B, CBA partnership in 2026
- Handles multi-country contract review out of the box

### Cons

- Pricing is opaque — exact costs require a demo call
- Hard lock-in to Microsoft Word; zero utility for Google Docs teams
- Not designed for litigation, legal research, or memo drafting
- At $89+/user/month, cost scales quickly for larger teams
- General-purpose AI tools with custom contract prompts can replicate roughly 80% of functionality at $20–25/month for budget-constrained users

## Who Should NOT Use Spellbook

- **Google Workspace teams:** Spellbook is a Word add-in only. If your firm runs on Google Docs, this tool simply does not apply.
- **Litigation attorneys:** Spellbook is built for transactional and contract work. It does not help with legal research, briefs, or courtroom preparation.
- **Solo practitioners with low contract volume:** At $89+/user/month, the ROI math only works if you are reviewing contracts frequently. A general-purpose AI with well-crafted contract prompts may serve you better at a fraction of the cost.
- **Teams needing full CLM platforms:** Spellbook focuses on the drafting and review layer. If you need end-to-end contract lifecycle management with approval workflows and obligation tracking, you will need a dedicated CLM platform alongside or instead of Spellbook.

## Verdict

Spellbook is the strongest purpose-built AI contract review tool for Word-native legal teams in 2026. Its playbook system, tracked-changes redlines, and clause benchmarking against 2,300+ contract types set it meaningfully apart from using a general-purpose AI chatbot for the same task. The April 2026 Series B and CBA exclusive partnership confirm this is a well-funded, stable product with real institutional adoption — not a startup risk.

The main friction points are pricing opacity and the hard requirement for Microsoft Word. If your team lives in Word and handles commercial contracts regularly, Spellbook delivers real time savings and the 7-day trial makes it easy to verify fit before committing. If you are cost-sensitive, platform-agnostic, or focused on non-transactional legal work, look elsewhere.

**Bottom line: 8.5/10 — best-in-class for Word-native contract teams; limited by platform lock-in and lack of pricing transparency.**

## FAQ

### Is Spellbook free?

Spellbook offers a 7-day free trial. After that it is a paid subscription. Third-party sources report pricing starting around $89/user/month billed annually, though the official rate card is only available via a demo call.

### What AI model does Spellbook use?

Spellbook is built primarily on OpenAI's GPT-4 family, with additional models from Anthropic and its own fine-tuning layers trained on legal contract data for more precise clause-level suggestions.

### Does Spellbook work in Google Docs?

No. Spellbook is a Microsoft Word add-in only. It does not support Google Docs, standalone web apps, or browser-based editors.

### What are the best Spellbook alternatives in 2026?

Leading alternatives include LEGALFLY (privacy-focused, also Microsoft 365 native), Harvey (research and memo drafting for large law firms), and HAQQ (multi-language support and multiple legal conventions). For budget-conscious users, Claude or ChatGPT with custom contract prompts can handle basic review at $20–25/month.

### Is Spellbook worth it for small law firms?

It can be, especially with a high volume of commercial contracts. The 7-day trial lets you test fit before committing. For firms with low contract volume, the per-user monthly cost may be hard to justify against general-purpose alternatives.
