## What is Intercom Fin?

Intercom Fin is an AI customer service agent built on top of the Intercom platform. It resolves inbound support tickets end to end across chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, Slack, and phone — without human involvement. Instead of a flat monthly bot fee, Fin uses outcome-based pricing: you pay only when it closes a ticket on its own.

Salesforce acquired Intercom in 2024, and that acquisition continues to shape buyer decisions in 2026, particularly for teams weighing long-term platform commitment.

## Key Features

- **Multi-channel resolution** — chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, Slack, and voice handled by one agent
- **Knowledge-base-driven answers** — Fin reads your help center articles, PDFs, and connected sources to generate responses
- **Human handoff** — escalates to a live agent when confidence is low or the customer requests it
- **Outcome-based billing** — $0.99 per resolved conversation, not per seat or per message
- **Native Intercom integration** — works inside existing Intercom inboxes with no separate dashboard
- **CSAT tracking** — built-in satisfaction scoring alongside resolution rate reporting

## Pricing

Intercom Fin uses a two-part pricing model as of August 2026:

- **$0.99 per resolved outcome** — charged only when Fin closes a ticket without human escalation
- **Seat plan from $29/seat/month** — an active Intercom plan is required; Fin layers on top of it

In practice, monthly Fin costs range from roughly $124 to $272 depending on ticket volume and knowledge base quality. The better your documentation, the more Fin resolves — and the more you pay, but also the more support cost you offset.

**Critical pricing caveat:** "Resolved" means Fin closed the conversation without a human joining — not that the customer's problem was genuinely solved. Teams with poor knowledge bases will see lower resolution rates, lower cost, and lower CSAT simultaneously.

## Real-World Resolution Rates

Intercom markets Fin as capable of resolving up to 50% of queries. Independent testing tells a more cautious story. A 60-day test across four small business clients (500 tickets/month combined) recorded an average resolution rate of 38%, not 50%. Across deployments, rates vary from 25% to 55%, driven primarily by knowledge base depth and query complexity.

For comparison, one B2C team (Freecash) reported 71% resolution using a competing AI integration on top of Intercom's inbox — suggesting Fin's ceiling is heavily constrained by documentation quality, not just the underlying model.

If you evaluate Fin, track CSAT alongside resolution rate. High resolution with dropping satisfaction scores signals Fin is closing tickets customers aren't happy with — a real risk when the billing model rewards closures rather than quality.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Pay-per-resolution keeps costs low during slow periods
- Genuine multi-channel coverage: chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, Slack, and voice
- Strong third-party rating: 4.5/5 across more than 2,900 G2 reviews
- Native to Intercom — minimal setup for existing customers
- Human handoff works reliably in most tested scenarios

### Cons

- "Resolved" does not equal customer satisfied — monitor CSAT separately
- Knowledge base drift degrades accuracy after every product update or pricing change
- Salesforce acquisition creates platform uncertainty for long-term buyers
- Real resolution rates (25–38% in small business testing) often fall short of the marketed 50%
- Requires an active Intercom seat plan on top of per-resolution fees

## Who Should NOT Use Intercom Fin

- **Teams without a maintained knowledge base** — Fin's accuracy drops fast if your help docs are outdated or thin
- **Very small teams with simple FAQ queries** — seat cost plus per-resolution fees may not justify setup overhead compared to a simpler chatbot
- **Companies concerned about Salesforce lock-in** — the acquisition is real and roadmap changes are possible; evaluate alternatives if platform independence is a priority
- **Teams that need guaranteed resolution quality** — if your SLAs depend on genuinely solved issues rather than closed conversations, Fin's resolution metric is not a reliable SLA proxy

## Verdict

Intercom Fin is a capable AI support agent for mid-sized teams already invested in the Intercom ecosystem. The $0.99-per-resolution model is genuinely fair — you only pay when it works. But "works" is narrower than the marketing implies: expect 25–45% real-world resolution, not 50%, and watch CSAT closely to ensure quality keeps pace with throughput.

The Salesforce acquisition is not a dealbreaker today, but it is a variable worth tracking before signing a multi-year deal. For teams with clean, up-to-date knowledge bases and moderate-to-high ticket volume, Fin pays for itself. For teams with thin documentation or complex query mixes, alternatives may deliver better ROI at a lower maintenance cost.

## FAQ

### How much does Intercom Fin cost in 2026?

Fin charges $0.99 per resolved conversation. You also need an active Intercom seat plan, which starts at $29/seat/month as of August 2026. Monthly totals typically fall between $124 and $272 depending on volume and how well your knowledge base is maintained.

### What resolution rate can I realistically expect from Intercom Fin?

Real-world testing puts the range at 25–55%, with small business accounts averaging closer to 38%. The marketed ceiling of 50%+ requires a well-maintained knowledge base and a query mix that skews toward documented, straightforward issues.

### Does Salesforce's acquisition of Intercom affect Fin?

The acquisition closed in 2024 and continues to influence buyer decisions in 2026. No major feature rollbacks have been confirmed, but teams evaluating long-term contracts should factor in potential roadmap changes under Salesforce's ownership.

### Is Intercom Fin right for small businesses?

It depends on your documentation quality and ticket volume. Small businesses with fewer than 200 tickets per month and thin help docs may find the combined seat and per-resolution cost less economical than a flat-fee alternative. Teams with well-maintained knowledge bases and higher volume generally get better value.

### How does Fin define a 'resolved' conversation?

Intercom counts a conversation as resolved when Fin closes it without human involvement — not when the customer confirms their problem is solved. This distinction matters: always track CSAT alongside resolution rate to catch cases where Fin closes tickets customers are still unhappy with.
