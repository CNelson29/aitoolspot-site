## What is HeyGen?

HeyGen is the category leader for AI avatar video generation. You type a script, pick a digital avatar, choose a language, and the platform renders a polished talking-head video in minutes — no camera, no studio, no on-screen talent required. The platform targets corporate L&D teams, content localizers, UGC-style ad creators, and developers who want to embed avatar video via API.

In 2026, HeyGen's main differentiators are its Avatar IV rendering engine, Video Agent 2.0 workflow automation, videos up to 30 minutes long, and multilingual re-lip-sync across 175+ languages — a combination no direct competitor fully matches yet.

## Key Features

- **Avatar IV:** HeyGen's latest rendering engine delivers improved lip-sync accuracy and more natural micro-expressions compared to earlier generations. It is the most realistic avatar option on the platform and the one most reviewers tested in 2026.
- **Video Agent 2.0:** An AI workflow layer that automates scripting, avatar selection, and scene sequencing — reducing manual setup time for recurring video formats like product walkthroughs or training modules.
- **175+ language support with re-lip-sync:** Translate an existing video into dozens of languages and HeyGen re-animates the avatar's mouth to match the new audio. This is one of the platform's strongest practical differentiators for global teams.
- **30-minute video length:** The longest supported duration among major AI avatar platforms as of mid-2026, making it viable for full training sessions and long-form onboarding content.
- **Developer API:** Embed avatar generation into your own product or automate video production pipelines without using the web interface.
- **Custom avatar creation:** Business and Enterprise plans allow teams to create a digital twin from a short recorded clip, enabling branded spokesperson avatars at scale.

## Pricing

HeyGen uses a credit-based billing system. Verified 2026 plan tiers are as follows:

- **Free — $0/month:** 3 videos per month, watermarked output. Sufficient for evaluation, not for production.
- **Creator — $29/month:** Unlimited video generations plus 200 monthly credits included.
- **Pro — $99/month:** Higher credit allocation and access to additional advanced features.
- **Business — $149/month + $20/seat:** Adds 4K rendering, custom avatar creation, and SSO for teams.
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing for high-volume organizations.

**The credit cost per minute is what actually determines your bill.** Avatar IV — the most realistic engine — costs 20 credits per minute of finished video. Depending on your plan, that puts Avatar IV production at roughly $0.97–$1.99 per finished minute. Avatar III uses only 3 credits per minute, a significant difference if you are producing at volume. Upgrading plans gives you more credits but does not reduce the per-minute credit rate, so high-volume Avatar IV users should model their monthly output before choosing a tier.
