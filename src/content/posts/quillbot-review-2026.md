## What is QuillBot?

QuillBot is an AI-powered writing suite built around a paraphrasing engine. What started as a simple rewriter has grown into a full toolkit covering grammar correction, summarization, plagiarism detection, citation generation, and translation across 45+ languages. In recent updates it added an AI Humanizer and an AI Detector, making it a credible one-stop shop for anyone who needs to write, clean up, or verify text.

The core audience is students and non-native English speakers who need to rephrase content without losing meaning — but content creators and professionals use it too, especially for grammar checking and summarization.

## Key Features

- **Paraphraser (9 modes)** — Standard, Fluency, Formal, Academic, Simple, Creative, Expand, Shorten, and Custom. Each mode reshapes text with a different goal.
- **Grammar Checker** — Catches grammar, punctuation, and style errors with real-time feedback.
- **Summarizer** — Condenses long documents into key points or a single paragraph.
- **Plagiarism Detector** — Checks text against web sources (Premium only).
- **Citation Generator** — Formats citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, and other styles automatically.
- **AI Humanizer** — Rewrites AI-generated text to read more naturally.
- **AI Detector** — Flags text likely written by an AI model.
- **Translator** — Translates across 45+ languages.
- **Browser Extension** — Works inside Google Docs, Gmail, and most text fields in Chrome.

## Pricing

QuillBot uses a freemium model. The **Free plan** caps each paraphrasing input at 125 words and limits access to three rewriting modes. Grammar checking and summarization are available on free, with reduced functionality.

The **Premium plan** costs **$4.17/month billed annually**. Premium unlocks unlimited paraphrasing, all nine rewriting modes, advanced grammar checks, plagiarism detection, the AI Humanizer, and priority processing. Monthly billing is available at a higher rate.

There is no time-limited free trial of Premium, but the free tier is substantial enough to evaluate the core paraphraser before committing. QuillBot does not offer a team or enterprise plan publicly advertised at time of writing — it is primarily a single-user product.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- Strong value at $4.17/month — among the lowest-priced full writing suites available
- Nine paraphrasing modes cover academic, creative, and professional needs in one tool
- 45+ language translation built in, no separate subscription needed
- Citation generator saves significant time for students and researchers
- Browser extension integrates directly into Google Docs and Gmail
- AI Humanizer useful for cleaning up AI-drafted content without switching tools
- Free tier is usable, not crippled — good for occasional rewrites

### Cons

- Free plan caps at 125 words per input — frustrating for anything longer than a paragraph
- Paraphrasing can drift from original meaning in Creative mode; always review output
- Plagiarism checker is Premium-only
- Not designed for generating long-form content from scratch
- No team workspaces or collaboration features
- AI Chat and Image Generator feel secondary to the core writing tools

## Who Should NOT Use QuillBot

- **Long-form content writers** who need to generate full articles from a brief — dedicated AI writers handle that better.
- **SEO professionals** who need keyword-targeted drafts rather than rewrites of existing text.
- **Teams needing shared workspaces** — QuillBot has no collaboration layer.
- **Users who want inline real-time suggestions** as they type — Grammarly's integration is more seamless for that workflow.
- **Anyone who needs sourced research** built into their writing tool — QuillBot does not pull live web data into drafts.

## Verdict

QuillBot earns its reputation as the go-to paraphrasing tool for students, non-native English speakers, and anyone who edits more than they draft. At $4.17/month, the Premium plan is difficult to beat on price — the nine rewriting modes, citation generator, and AI Humanizer cover real workflows that other tools charge significantly more for, separately.

Where it falls short is long-form generation and collaboration. If you need to write content from scratch at scale or work in a team, look elsewhere. But if your primary job is rewriting, cleaning up, summarizing, or checking text, QuillBot delivers the most per dollar of any writing suite in 2026.

**Rating: 8.5 / 10**

## FAQ

### Is QuillBot free?

Yes. QuillBot has a free plan that supports up to 125 words per paraphrasing input and includes grammar checking and summarization with limited functionality. No credit card is required.

### How much does QuillBot Premium cost?

QuillBot Premium costs $4.17/month when billed annually. Monthly billing is available at a higher rate. There is no team or enterprise pricing publicly listed.

### Does QuillBot detect AI content?

Yes. QuillBot includes an AI Detector to flag AI-written text and an AI Humanizer to rewrite AI content so it reads more naturally — both are available on Premium.

### Can QuillBot translate text?

Yes. QuillBot supports translation across 45+ languages directly within the platform.

### Is QuillBot good for academic writing?

QuillBot is widely used in academic contexts. The Academic paraphrasing mode, citation generator (APA, MLA, Chicago), and plagiarism checker (Premium) make it practical for students and researchers. Always verify paraphrased output before submitting work.

### How does QuillBot compare to Grammarly?

QuillBot is stronger at paraphrasing and summarization; Grammarly is stronger at inline real-time editing and tone suggestions. They serve overlapping but distinct use cases. QuillBot Premium is generally cheaper.
