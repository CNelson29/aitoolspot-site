## What is Apollo.io?

Apollo.io is an all-in-one B2B sales intelligence and engagement platform. It bundles a database of 275M+ contacts with AI-powered email sequencing, a built-in dialer, intent data signals, deal management, and CRM integrations — giving revenue teams a single tool where most outbound stacks used to require four or five. In 2026 it remains one of the most widely used outbound platforms among SDRs, AEs, and solo sellers looking to consolidate their tooling.

## Key Features

- **Contact & Company Database:** 275M+ verified contacts filterable by job title, industry, location, company size, funding stage, and technology used. The database is the primary reason most teams choose Apollo over point solutions.
- **AI Sequences & Email Automation:** Multi-step outreach cadences across email and phone. Apollo's AI suggests personalized copy, optimizes send times, and handles follow-ups automatically based on prospect behavior.
- **Intent Data:** Identifies companies actively researching topics relevant to your offer, giving you a timing advantage when reaching out to in-market buyers — a feature competitors often charge extra for.
- **Built-in Dialer:** Make and log calls without leaving the platform. Call recordings and transcripts are stored directly in Apollo alongside contact records.
- **AI Lead Scoring & Recommendations:** Apollo's AI scores leads on fit signals and flags churn risk for existing accounts, suggesting the best next action to convert or retain them.
- **CRM Sync:** Native two-way integrations with Salesforce and HubSpot keep contact and activity data synchronized automatically, reducing manual entry.
- **Bulk Enrichment:** Fill gaps in existing CRM records with up-to-date data from Apollo's database in bulk — useful for teams inheriting stale contact lists.

## Pricing

Apollo.io offers four plan tiers, starting with a free plan and scaling to an Organization tier. Paid plans run approximately **$59 to $149 per seat per month** depending on features and credit volume. The free plan includes limited export credits and basic prospecting, making it accessible for solo operators evaluating the platform before committing. Custom enterprise pricing is available on request.

*Note: Pricing is subject to change. Verify current rates on Apollo.io's official pricing page before purchasing.*

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

- 275M+ contact database with granular filtering options
- All-in-one: prospecting, sequencing, dialing, and enrichment under one roof
- Free plan available — no credit card required to test core features
- AI sequences reduce time spent on manual personalization at scale
- Strong native CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Intent data included in platform — not a paid add-on
- Active AI roadmap with frequent feature releases through 2025–2026

### Cons

- Data accuracy is imperfect — bounces and stale contacts exist in the database
- Not a true CRM replacement at scale; complex pipelines still need a dedicated CRM
- Initial setup can feel overwhelming for small teams new to structured outbound
- Advanced AI features and higher credit volumes are locked behind paid tiers
- Support responsiveness is a recurring complaint across user reviews

## Who Should NOT Use Apollo.io

- **Inbound-only teams:** If your pipeline is entirely inbound, most of Apollo's features go unused and the investment is hard to justify.
- **Teams requiring near-perfect data accuracy:** Apollo's database is large but not error-free. For high-volume sends where deliverability is critical, pair it with a dedicated email verifier before blasting.
- **Businesses needing a full CRM:** Apollo is a prospecting and engagement layer, not a deal management system at the depth of Salesforce or HubSpot for complex enterprise pipelines.
- **AI SDR-first workflows:** If you want a fully autonomous AI SDR running your entire outbound motion end-to-end, platforms like Reply.io offer more mature AI agent automation specifically in that direction.

## Verdict

Apollo.io earns its position as one of the top B2B outbound tools in 2026. The combination of a 275M+ contact database, AI-driven sequencing, intent data, and a built-in dialer — at a price point that replaces multiple standalone tools — is genuinely hard to beat. It is especially strong for SDRs, AEs, and growth-stage teams that need to move fast on outbound without managing a fragmented tool stack.

The main caveat is data quality: Apollo's database is extensive but imperfect, and teams with strict deliverability requirements should validate contacts before large sends. If you can work within that trade-off — and most outbound teams can — Apollo.io is a highly competitive, well-rounded choice for 2026.

## FAQ

### Is Apollo.io free to use?

Yes. Apollo.io offers a free plan with limited credits and basic prospecting features. Paid plans start at approximately $59 per seat per month for higher credit volumes and access to AI automation features.

### How accurate is Apollo.io's contact database?

Apollo's 275M+ contact database is one of the largest available for B2B outbound, but accuracy is not perfect. Users regularly report a percentage of bounces and outdated records. For campaigns where deliverability is critical, running contacts through a dedicated email verifier before large sends is advisable.

### Does Apollo.io integrate with HubSpot and Salesforce?

Yes. Apollo.io has native two-way sync integrations with both HubSpot and Salesforce, keeping contact and activity data updated across platforms automatically without manual export/import workflows.

### Who are the main Apollo.io alternatives in 2026?

The most commonly cited alternatives are ZoomInfo (enterprise-scale database), Lusha (simpler UI with higher precision focus), Reply.io (stronger AI SDR agent features), and Salesgenie. The right pick depends on team size, budget, and whether you prioritize database size, data accuracy, or AI automation depth.

### Is Apollo.io good for solo sellers?

Yes. The free plan and lower-tier paid options make Apollo.io accessible for individual sellers. It is particularly well-suited for solo operators and small teams running outbound without a dedicated sales ops function.
