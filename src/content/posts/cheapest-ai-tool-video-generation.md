## What Are the Cheapest AI Video Generators in 2026?

If you are hunting for the cheapest AI tool for video generation, you are not alone. The market expanded sharply in 2026, and so did pricing complexity — credits, subscriptions, unlimited tiers, and per-generation fees make it genuinely hard to compare platforms. This guide cuts through the noise and ranks the most affordable options by real cost per video.

We examined five leading tools: **OpenArt**, **Hailuo (MiniMax)**, **Pika**, **Veo**, and **WAN 2.6** — covering pay-per-use rates, free tiers, and monthly subscription pricing sourced from third-party reviewers and the tools themselves.

## Key Features

### OpenArt — Cheapest Pay-Per-Use Rate

OpenArt is the most affordable option on a per-generation basis, coming in at approximately **3 cents per generation** — the lowest rate tracked across major platforms in 2026. It supports text-to-video and image-to-video workflows and suits creators who generate video in bursts rather than daily volume.

### Hailuo (MiniMax Hailuo H3) — Best Free Tier with Strong Motion

Hailuo offers a generous free tier and paid plans starting at **$9.99/month**. Litmus (reviewed June 2026) rates it 80/100 with a value score of 9.0/10. It features director-mode camera controls and strong motion quality, making it a go-to for budget-minded creators needing physical B-roll or short animated clips. Note: Hailuo is China-hosted and commercial licensing terms are limited — review them before publishing content commercially.

### Pika — Best Free Tier for Experimentation

Pika's free tier includes **80 credits per month** on its Pika 2.5 model — enough to properly test the tool before committing money. AIToolTier rates it 7.8/10, with ease of use at 9.0/10 and value at 8.0/10. It is positioned as a fast, accessible tool that is easier on the wallet than Runway without sacrificing usability.

### Veo — Lowest Subscription Entry Price

Google's Veo offers free access alongside paid plans starting at **$4.99/month** — one of the lowest subscription entry points in the category. PCMag highlights Veo's Flow tool for filmmakers, enabling cohesive, cinematic video creation. For creators who want a high-pedigree model at a low monthly rate, Veo's entry tier is the standout on price.

### WAN 2.6 — Zero Cost If Self-Hosted

Alibaba's WAN 2.6 is an open-source video model with strong multi-shot storytelling and audio-visual sync. If you have access to cloud GPU infrastructure or a capable local machine, running WAN 2.6 brings your per-generation cost close to zero. Amrytt rates it 4.3/5 overall with video quality at 4.5/5 and value at 4.5/5. The tradeoff: it requires technical setup and is not plug-and-play.

- **Users with strict data residency or IP requirements** — Hailuo is hosted in China; review terms carefully for commercial content
- **Non-technical users wanting zero setup** — WAN 2.6 requires self-hosting and GPU configuration

## Verdict

The cheapest AI tool for video generation in 2026 depends on your workflow:

- **Lowest per-generation cost:** OpenArt at ~$0.03/generation
- **Lowest monthly subscription:** Veo at $4.99/month

## FAQ

### What is the absolute cheapest AI video tool right now?

On a per-generation basis, OpenArt is the cheapest at approximately $0.03 per generation. For subscriptions, Veo starts at $4.99/month — the lowest monthly entry price among the tools reviewed here.

### Are there free AI video generators?

Yes. Veo, Pika, and Hailuo all offer free tiers. Pika gives 80 credits per month on its Pika 2.5 model. WAN 2.6 is fully open-source and free to run on your own hardware or a cloud GPU service.

### How do AI video generator credits work?

Credits vary per platform — one credit might buy a 5-second clip on one tool and a 10-second clip on another. Always check what a credit buys before committing to a plan. Unlimited plans typically carry fair-use caps, so read the fine print. OpenArt's ~$0.03 per generation rate is a useful baseline for comparison-shopping.

### Is Hailuo safe for commercial use?

Hailuo is developed by MiniMax, a China-based company. Multiple reviewers note that its commercial licensing terms are limited. Review the current terms of service on Hailuo's official website before using generated content for commercial projects.

### How do I compare AI video tool costs fairly?

Calculate cost per generation rather than comparing headline prices. A $10/month plan with 50 generations costs $0.20 per video, while a $30/month unlimited plan may cost less if you generate regularly. Always factor in resolution caps and clip length limits, which free and entry tiers commonly impose.
